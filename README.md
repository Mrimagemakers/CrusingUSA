# Cruising USA VR

A browser racing game built with Three.js and WebXR. Race one continuous cross-country highway through twelve American courses, from a Pacific Coast sunrise to the Alaskan aurora, on screen or in a VR headset.

**Play:** https://mrimagemakers.github.io/CrusingUSA/

## Features
- Twelve connected courses with their own scenery, weather and time of day
- Race, Cruise and 12 short Events with stars and prizes
- Seven rides, including four hypercars, unlocked through driver levels
- Garage paint, liveries, race numbers, performance upgrades and race tools
- Obstacles, pickups and boost pads on the road
- WebXR cockpit mode for headsets such as Meta Quest
- Graphics levels: LOW (phones and headsets), HIGH and ULTRA, with automatic step-down

## Controls
- Steer: A/D or ←/→ · Gas: W/↑ · Brake: S/↓ · Boost: Space
- Camera: C · Autopilot: P · Weather: T · Full screen: F · Pause: Esc
- Phones: on-screen buttons with auto gas
- VR: right trigger gas, left trigger brake, thumbstick steer, grip boost

The whole game is a single `index.html` file; it loads three.js r128 from cdnjs.
